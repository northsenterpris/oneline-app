How do I restore a purchase?

There are no in-app purchases in this version of One Line, so there is nothing to restore.

How do I delete my data?

Your entries are stored only on your device. We never receive them. To delete all of them, delete the app. Entries may also be in your device backups, such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup. Delete those backups too if you want the entries gone from them.

To turn off anonymous usage analytics, see the Privacy Policy.

Can I export my entries?

There is no text export at the moment. You can share your “Year in Lines” image, which shows which days you wrote and the mood colour for each day, using the share button on the Year in Lines screen. The image does not include any entry text.
