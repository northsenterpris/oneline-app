Privacy Policy — One Line – Gratitude Journal

Effective date: [DATE]

One Line – Gratitude Journal (“One Line”, “the app”) is made by Mia Ipsen, an independent developer based in Denmark. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what choices you have.

Short version: Your journal entries stay on your device. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking. If you agree to it, the app sends anonymous usage statistics so it can be improved. It also sends crash reports so bugs can be fixed.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The data controller is:

Mia Ipsen
Denmark
Email: ipsenmia90@gmail.com

2. Your journal entries stay on your device

Everything you write in One Line is stored only on your device. This includes your daily line, your mood tag, your streak and your history. We never receive, read, store or transmit it. There is no account and no server holding your journal.

Your entries may be included in your device’s normal backups, such as iCloud Backup or a computer backup, depending on your device settings. Those backups are handled by Apple or by you under your own settings, not by us.

3. Data we do collect

a) Usage analytics (only with your consent)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used. This includes things like which screens are opened, whether an entry was saved, app version and device type. Analytics never includes the text of your entries or your mood selections tied to your writing.

  • If you are in the EU/EEA, UK or Switzerland: analytics is off by default. It is only switched on if you agree in the consent form shown when you first open the app. You can change your choice at any time in the app’s Settings.
  • Outside those regions: analytics is on by default, as permitted by local law.

Data collected may include an app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, app version, country (derived from IP address), and app interaction events. This data is not linked to your name, email or identity, and is not used for advertising or tracking.

Legal basis (GDPR): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a)). You can withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Retention: analytics data is kept for [2 months] and then deleted automatically.

b) Crash reports

We use Firebase Crashlytics to receive reports when the app crashes. A crash report contains technical information such as the stack trace, device model, OS version, app version and an installation identifier. It never contains your journal entries.

Legal basis (GDPR): our legitimate interest in keeping the app stable and fixing bugs (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Retention: crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

c) Consent choices

To show the consent form and remember your choice, the app uses Google’s User Messaging Platform. It stores your consent choice on your device and may process your IP address to determine your region.

4. Sharing your “Year in Lines” image

The app lets you create an image showing which days you wrote and the mood colour for each day. The image does not include any entry text. It is created on your device and only leaves it if you choose to share it through the system share sheet. Where it goes then depends on the app or person you share it with.

5. Reminders

Daily reminders are scheduled locally on your device. No notification data is sent to us or to any server.

6. Who processes data on our behalf

Analytics and crash reporting are provided by Google (Google Ireland Limited for users in the EU/EEA; Google LLC elsewhere). Google acts as our data processor. Data may be transferred to and processed in the United States. These transfers are protected by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

Google’s privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell, rent or share your personal data with anyone else, and we do not use it for advertising.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR (and similar laws elsewhere) you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you
  • have it corrected or deleted
  • restrict or object to its processing
  • data portability
  • withdraw consent at any time

Because analytics and crash data are not linked to your identity, we usually cannot identify which data belongs to you. If you contact us, we will help as far as we reasonably can (GDPR Art. 11).

You can always:

  • withdraw analytics consent in the app’s Settings
  • delete all your entries by deleting the app (and any device backups that contain it)

To exercise your rights, email ipsenmia90@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

If you are in Denmark or elsewhere in the EU, you can also complain to a data protection authority. In Denmark this is Datatilsynet (www.datatilsynet.dk).

8. Children

One Line is not directed at children under 13. In EU countries where the age of digital consent is higher (up to 16), users below that age should only use the app with a parent’s or guardian’s permission. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the effective date above. Significant changes will be noted in the app’s release notes.
